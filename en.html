H. COHEN LAW FIRM

H. Cohen Law Firm was founded in 2001 by Adv. Hagai P. Cohen and incorporated as a law company in 2008. From the outset it has represented local authorities and public bodies, and over the years it has focused on legal counsel to Israel's religious councils and burial societies.

Today the firm serves as legal counsel to dozens of religious councils across Israel and to the burial societies operating within them. Since 2007 it has also handled the enforcement of municipal tax and water charges for local authorities and water corporations.

Practice areas

  • Religious services and burial
  • Public-sector employment law
  • Administrative law
  • Public tenders and procurement
  • Regulation and kashrut
  • Enforcement of payment obligations for local authorities and water corporations
  • Notarial services
